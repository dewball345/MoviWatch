2. conseguir = to get / to obtain
3. seguir = to follow / to continue
4. repetir = to repeat
5. dormir = to sleep
6. volver = to return / to come back
7. almorzar = to have lunch / to eat lunch
8. encontrar = to find
9. pensar = to think
10. querer = to want / to love
11. preferir = to prefer
12. entender = to understand
13. perder = to lose / to miss
14. empezar = to begin / to start
15. cerrar = to close

PART 6 - Escribir (SAMPLE)

proximo fin de semana queremos ir a la playa porque el clima es bonito.

WHAT THE TEACHER WANTED HER TO PRACTICE
- Ser vs. estar USAGE: ser = permanent/identity (origin, profession,
  personality, traits, dates, event time). estar = temporary/location
  (feelings, conditions, health, where something is).
- Knowing what each verb MEANS, then using it in a real sentence.
- Building sentences with o->ue and e->ie stem-changers.
For every miss, write the reason next to it and redo it.
