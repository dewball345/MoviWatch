HOW TO GRADE YOURSELF
- Parts 1-5, 7, Bonus: exact answers (count each blank).
- Part 6 & 8: check that the verb is conjugated correctly,
  agrees with the subject, and any 2nd verb stays infinitive.
- For every one you miss, write the rule next to it and redo
  it from scratch. That's where the real learning happens.
